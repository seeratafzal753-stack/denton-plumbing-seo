## Denton, TX Slab Leak Detection & Non-Invasive Repair

A slab leak occurs when a pressurized water line running beneath your home's concrete foundation develops a fracture or pinhole leak. Left unchecked, sub-slab water erosion can compromise your home's foundation footing and lead to massive repair costs.

At **Denton Plumbing Pros**, we combine state-of-the-art non-invasive acoustic technology with decades of North Texas soil plumbing experience to diagnose and fix foundation leaks with surgical precision.

### Modern Slab Leak Repair Strategies

* **PEX Overhead Pipe Reroute (Recommended):** We abandon the compromised underground copper pipe and route a brand new, highly durable PEX line through your attic and interior walls. This preserves your flooring and ensures that line will never leak under the slab again.
* **Direct Slab Spot Repair:** For accessible areas or single-story direct breaks, we carefully excavate a small access point through the foundation, replace the broken pipe section with commercial-grade copper/PEX fittings, and reseal the foundation vapor barrier.
