### The Impact of Denton's Hard Water on Water Heaters

Denton's tap water contains dissolved calcium and magnesium minerals. When water is heated inside your tank:
1. **Sediment Settlement:** Minerals crystallize and settle at the bottom of the tank.
2. **Burnout:** The heating elements or burner must burn through an insulating layer of mineral sediment, overheating the steel tank.
3. **Popping Sounds:** Trapped water boils under the mineral layer, creating distinct kettle-popping or rumbling noises.
4. **Premature Tank Rupture:** Over time, sediment causes the bottom of the tank to rust through, resulting in severe water leaks.

### Gas vs. Electric Water Heater Solutions

* **Gas Water Heaters:** Local specialists diagnose gas control valves, thermocouples, pilot assemblies, venting pipes, and emergency gas shutoff valves to prevent hazardous conditions.
* **Electric Water Heaters:** Contractors test and replace upper and lower heating elements, thermostats, circuit breakers, and reset switches.
* **Tankless Water Heaters:** Independent technicians perform chemical descaling flushes, sensor repairs, and complete whole-home tankless retrofits.

### Denton City Code Compliance & Safety

When installing a replacement water heater in Denton, TX, proper safety components are standard practice:
* **Thermal Expansion Tanks:** Protects your home's closed plumbing system from high thermal pressure spikes.
* **Temperature & Pressure Relief (T&P) Valves:** Discharges excess pressure safely to prevent catastrophic tank failure.
* **Sediment Traps & Gas Drip Legs:** Keeps debris from entering the burner assembly.
